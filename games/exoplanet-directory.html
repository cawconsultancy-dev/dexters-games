Facts come from NASA's Exoplanet Archive (October 2026). For the few worlds NASA does not list as confirmed, they come from the Open Exoplanet Catalogue, exoplanet.eu and the scientists' own papers. Temperatures for planets outside our Solar System are worked out from how much heat they get from their star. Nobody has a photo of these planets yet, so every picture here is drawn by the app as a best guess from its temperature and size. The stars, moons, galaxies, spacecraft and other new worlds (added November 2026) use the pictures from Dexter's Space Studio, and their facts are rounded.
